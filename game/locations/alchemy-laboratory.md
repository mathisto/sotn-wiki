## Boss: Slogra and Gaibon

**Location:** Room (7,22)

### Characteristics
- **Slogra**: Stabs or shoots fireballs with spear. After taking damage, drops spear and attacks with sharp mouth
- **Gaibon**: Shoots fireballs diagonally downward. Occasionally snatches Slogra for aerial drop attack. Turns red when damaged and shoots faster

### Strategy
- Focus on one boss first (Slogra is easier)
- Leather Shield blocks fireballs
- Axe sub-weapon is effective when both are airborne
