# Final Stage: Bloodlines

**Game**: Castlevania: Symphony of the Night  
**Japanese Name**: Final Stage: 血の輪廻 (Rondo of Blood)  
**Boss**: Dracula  
**Music**: Prologue

## Overview

The prologue stage where **Richter Belmont** faces **Dracula** - a reenactment of Rondo of Blood's finale.

## The Dracula Fight

### Iconic Dialogue

> **Richter**: Die monster. You don't belong in this world!  
> **Dracula**: It was not by my hand that I am once again given flesh. I was called here by humans who wish to pay me tribute.  
> **Richter**: Tribute!?! You steal men's souls and make them your slaves!  
> **Dracula**: Perhaps the same could be said of all religions...  
> **Richter**: Your words are as empty as your soul! Mankind ill needs a savior such as you!  
> **Dracula**: What is a man? *(throws wine glass)* A miserable little pile of secrets. But enough talk... Have at you!
