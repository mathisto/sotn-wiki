# Death Wing's Lair

**Japanese Name:** 死翼の間 (Shiyoku no Ma) - "Chambers of the Death Wing"

**Game:** Castlevania: Symphony of the Night (Reverse Castle)

## Description

Death Wing's Lair is the inverted (upside-down) version of **Olrox's Quarters** in the Reverse Castle. The identity of "Death Wing" is unclear—it does not refer to the area's boss.

## Boss: Akmodan II

The Mummy boss of this area.

## Trivia

- **"Death Wing's Lair"** may be a mistranslation:
  - Could refer to a castle section ("wing") called the Death Wing
  - Could reference the many Karasuman (crow demons) in the courtyard
