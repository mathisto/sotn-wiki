# Floating Catacombs

**Japanese Name:** 空中墓地 (*Kūchū Bochi* - Aerial Cemetery)

> Powerful demons from a distant future inhabit this glacial tomb stretching beyond space and time.

## Overview

The Floating Catacombs are an upside-down version of the Catacombs in the Reverse Castle. This area has the most drastic environmental changes:

- Lava appears as molten snow
- Snowflakes drift down in some areas
- Rocks have formed into crystals
- Green bricks are now menacing red

This is the **highest stage in the game**, accessible only through the Cave. Entirely optional.

## Boss: Galamoth

One of the toughest bosses in the game.

**Attacks:**
- Throws large shadowy balls
- Uses rod to electrify the player

**Strategies:**
1. **Shield Rod + Alucard Shield combo** - Drains health quickly
2. **Beryl Circlet** - Negates electrical attacks

**Reward:** Gas Cloud relic (enhanced Mist Form with damage)
