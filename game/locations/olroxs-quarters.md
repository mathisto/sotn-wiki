## Overview

Olrox's Quarters is a section of Dracula's Castle devoted to one of Dracula's closest allies, **Olrox**. The area features:

- Dark, elegant hallways transitioning to dungeon-like corridors on the top floor
- A large moonlit room housing what appears to be an abandoned village
- A beautiful flowing decorated water fountain that gradually turns blood-red when approached

### Navigation

1. **Entry**: Reached through the Room of Clocks in the Marble Gallery
2. **Stairway**: Large stairs populated with Skelerangs lead to an open chamber dominated by the Spectral Sword
3. **Left path**: Leads to the Colosseum
4. **Right path**: Long hallway guarded by the giant brothers Hammer and Blade; ends at the Form of Bat relic
5. **Upper areas**: Require Form of Bat to traverse vertical sections leading to dungeons
6. **Villa area**: Below and to the left via a broken bridge
7. **Boss chamber**: At the end of the broken bridge above the villa
