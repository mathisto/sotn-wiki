# Long Library

**Japanese Name:** 蔵書庫 (*Zōshoko* - "Library")

## Overview

The Long Library is the fifth stage in *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night*. It's a place where enchanted spell books attack visitors, bookcases packed with books line the walls, and shady spots for reading are off to the side.

The Library is essential for progression - players must visit the **Master Librarian** to purchase the **Jewel of Open** ($500), which unlocks blue doors throughout the castle. This grants access to:
- **Underground Caverns** (via Marble Gallery)
- **Royal Chapel** (via Alchemy Laboratory)

The corresponding Reverse Castle stage is the **Forbidden Library**.

### Connections
- **Previous Stage:** Outer Wall (only connected area)
- **Music:** Wood Carving Partita

---

## The Master Librarian

An old acquaintance of Alucard who resides in the Long Library. Despite his loyalty to Dracula, he's willing to help - for a price.

### First Encounter Dialogue

> **Alucard:** It's been a long time, old one....  
> **Librarian:** Oh it's you, Master Alucard. What do you need?  
> **Alucard:** I need your help.  
> **Librarian:** Young master, I cannot aid one who opposes the Master.  
> **Alucard:** You won't go unrewarded...  
> **Librarian:** Really? In that case just tell me what you need.
