# Marble Gallery

**Japanese Name:** 大理石の廊下 (Dairiseki no Rōka)  
**Game:** Castlevania: Symphony of the Night  
**Music:** "Marble Gallery"

## Overview

The Marble Gallery is the third stage in Symphony of the Night. It's a large, ornate transition area connecting to multiple zones of the castle. Notable features include:

- The Clock Room with a grand clock
- Hallways filled with grandfather clocks
- The longest corridor in the game, where a large floating eyeball can be seen through far windows
- Marble statues and paintings throughout

## Secrets

### Clock Room Mechanics
1. **Left Statue:** Opens automatically over time - leads to Olrox's Quarters (requires Double Jump)
2. **Right Statue:** Use Stopwatch sub-weapon to freeze time and open passage to Alucart equipment
3. **Floor:** With Gold Ring + Silver Ring equipped, floor opens to Ceremonial Room (contains Holy Glasses needed for true ending)

### The Floating Eyeball
In the long eastern corridor, a giant floating eyeball can be seen through the windows. This is purely atmospheric and cannot be interacted with.

## Story Event

**First Encounter with Maria Renard** occurs in the Clock Room:
- Maria notices Alucard has dark power but appears human
- Both reveal they intend to destroy the castle
- They introduce themselves before Maria departs
