**Castle:** Normal Castle (Area 14)  
**Connected To:** Abandoned Mine

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Save Rooms** | (28,45) and (20,45) |
| **Warp Room** | None |
| **Exit** | (30,44) to Abandoned Mine |
| **Boss Location** | Granfaloon at (17,45) |

### Requirements
- **Leap Stone** - Required to fight Granfaloon
- **Echo of Bat** - Required to obtain Spike Breaker (navigate dark spike room)

## Boss: Granfaloon

A massive creature consisting of a core surrounded by nine corpse shells.

**Mechanics:**
- Shells continuously drop walking corpses
- As shells break, core reveals tentacles that fire lasers
- If all shells destroyed, fires homing rays between laser attacks

**Strategy:**
- Use Axe or Cross sub-weapons for easier hits
- Clear walking corpses when overwhelmed
- Keep at least one shell intact to avoid homing rays

**Reward:** Access to Mormegil (dark element sword)
