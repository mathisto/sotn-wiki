# Underground Caverns - Castlevania: Symphony of the Night

**Japanese Name:** 地下水脈 (Chika Suimyaku) - "Ground Water Vein"

**Environment:** Cave, Crystal Caves, Flooding Area

**Music:** Crystal Teardrop

---

## Overview

The Underground Caverns is a melancholic, subterranean environment featuring icy walls, small rivers, and a large waterfall. Purple-glowing stalactites and stalagmites fill the distance.

### Navigation
- **Entry:** Through an opening in the Marble Gallery floor (requires Jewel of Open)
- **Two paths:** Right path is a "long drop"; left path is a basement section
- **Optional boss:** Scylla (right of long drop)
- **Hidden passage:** Wooden floor destroyed by Skeleton Ape's barrel leads to Abandoned Pit to the Catacomb
- **Ferryman:** Appears with Merman Statue; transports through waterways
- **Holy Symbol:** Prevents water damage

### Connected Areas
- Entrance
- Marble Gallery
- Abandoned Pit to the Catacomb
- Reverse Castle counterpart: Reverse Caverns
