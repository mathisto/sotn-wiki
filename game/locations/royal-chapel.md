# Royal Chapel

**Game**: Castlevania: Symphony of the Night  
**Japanese Name**: 礼拝堂 (*Reihaidō* - Chapel)  
**Environment**: Chapel / Great Stairway  
**Music**: Requiem for the Gods

## Location Overview

The Royal Chapel is the sixth stage in Symphony of the Night. A place of worship that seems out of place in Dracula's evil castle, featuring:

- Large bell towers and long stairways
- Stained glass windows
- Long rows of pews
- Cathedral-like main chapel area

## Special Features

### Confessional Room
A ghostly priest will listen to confessions and may:
- Give Grape Juice (Wine in Japanese version)
- Hurt the player
- Leave after crossing himself

**Warning Signs**: Grey robe + red eyes = attack incoming

Alucard can also take the priest's place, where a confessor will either confess sins (red) or attack (blue-green).

## Story Events

### Third Encounter with Maria
*After defeating Hippogryph*

Maria asks about Richter Belmont, who disappeared a year ago. Alucard agrees to let her know if he sees him.

### Fourth Encounter with Maria  
*After crossing spike room*

Alucard reveals he found a Belmont who claims to be the lord of the castle. Maria refuses to believe this and leaves in distress, dropping the **Silver Ring**.
