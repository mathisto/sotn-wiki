# Entrance (Symphony of the Night)

**Japanese Name:** 城入り口 (*Shiro Iriguchi* - Castle Entrance)

## Overview

The Entrance is the first stage in *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night*, entered as the hero crosses the drawbridge in the opening sequence. It leads into the Alchemy Laboratory. Its counterpart in the Reverse Castle is the Reverse Entrance.

**Theme Music:** Dracula's Castle
