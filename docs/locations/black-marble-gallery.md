**Location**: Inverted Castle  
**Normal Castle Counterpart**: Marble Gallery

| Exit | Connects To |

## Notable Features

- **Guardian enemies** - Strongest regular enemies in the game (4 total in clock corridors)
- **Reversed Giant Clock** - Same mechanics as normal castle
- **Pop-up spike traps** - NOT countered by Spike Breaker armor
- **Final Boss Access** - Requires all 5 Vlad pieces
