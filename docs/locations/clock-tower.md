# Clock Tower (Symphony of the Night)

## Overview

The **Clock Tower** (時計塔, *Tokeidō*) is a location in *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night*. It is accessible from the Outer Wall and leads to the Castle Keep.

- **Japanese Name**: 時計塔 (*Tokeidō*)
- **Environment**: Clock tower
- **Music Track**: The Tragic Prince
- **Previous Area**: Outer Wall
- **Next Area**: Castle Keep
- **Reverse Castle Counterpart**: Reverse Clock Tower

## Location Details

### Key Features
- **No Save Rooms** - The only area in Symphony of the Night without any save points
- **Spike Hazards** - Spikes deal damage scaled with player's max HP
- **Recreation of Rondo of Blood Stage 7** - Similar layout including the boss room

### Secrets

#### Gear Puzzle
Four gears in the main tower (two in each chamber). Hit each gear until it makes a **small click** (not a loud clank) to raise the gate in the lower-left of the tower. This reveals a secret room containing:
- Steel Helm
- Gold Plate
- Star Flail

#### Hidden Rooms
| Location | How to Access | Contents |
|----------|---------------|----------|
| Upper-right left-hand room | Destroy wall | Chamber with Flea Armors → larger "top of tower" room |
| Top of tower room | Through Flea Armor chamber | HP Max Up, Heart Max Up (guarded by Cloaked Knight); Rainbow Robe (Saturn only) |
| Pendulum room (lower-left) | Destroy slab with visible seams | Healing Mail |
| Pendulum room (right alcove) | Breakable wall | Pot Roast |
| Final corridor to boss | Various walls | Throwable items |

## Trivia
