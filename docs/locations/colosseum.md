# Colosseum - Castlevania: Symphony of the Night

## Location Overview

The **Colosseum** is a location in *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night*. It is an ancient arena within Dracula's Castle featuring gladiatorial architecture, broken pillars, and challenging combat encounters.

### Connections
| Direction | Area |
|-----------|------|
| West | Olrox's Quarters |
| East | Royal Chapel |

### Layout
- **Lower area**: Filled with tough enemies and valuable treasure
- **Upper area**: Contains the boss fight and the Form of Mist relic
- **Hidden area**: Secret ceiling passage right of two broken pillars (requires flight ability)

---

## Boss: Minotaur & Werewolf

**Dual Boss Fight** - You face both the Minotaur and Werewolf simultaneously in the arena.

- **Strategy**: Focus on one enemy at a time; Werewolf is faster but weaker
- **Reward**: Form of Mist relic upon victory

## Walkthrough Notes

1. **Access**: Enter from Olrox's Quarters (requires passing through Marble Gallery and Clock Room)
2. **Clock Room**: Wait for the left statue to move (~1 minute intervals), then double-jump through
3. **Exploration**: Clear lower area first for treasure
4. **Boss**: Defeat Minotaur & Werewolf in upper arena
5. **Relic**: Obtain Form of Mist - enables progress to Long Library
6. **Return later**: Come back with flight ability to get Holy Sword from hidden ceiling area

---

## Tips

- Use **Stopwatch** sub-weapon against Spectral Swords
- The Form of Mist is essential for game progression
- Mark this area for backtracking once you obtain the Bat/Gravity Boots
