## Major Glitches/Skips

> **Warning**: Some techniques are not allowed in all speedrun categories. Check specific category rules.

### Death Skip

Allows Alucard to keep his starting equipment (which Death normally takes). Multiple methods exist:

| Method | How to Perform |
|--------|----------------|
| **Neutron Bomb** | 1. Kill Zombies to get 88-99 XP before final Warg. 2. Let Warg charge left, break block revealing Turkey. 3. Stand at stair edge, equip Neutron Bomb. 4. Activate bomb while walking left at precise pixel position. 5. Hold Dash during Level Up animation. 6. Full jump right over stairs into next room. 7. **Critical**: Don't fall back into Death's room (causes soft-lock). |
| **Big Toss** | Requires Luck Mode. *(Details pending)* |
| **Spell** | Uses Level Up animation like Neutron Bomb method. *(Details pending)* |
| **Lycan** | Randomizer only. *(Details pending)* |
| **Wing Smash** | Randomizer only. *(Details pending)* |
